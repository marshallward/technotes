===========================
Implementation of ``exp()``
===========================

Steps to implement an exponent function.


Range reduction
===============

Generally start by substitution of :math:`exp()` in floating point form,
:math:`(-1)^s 2^n (1 + \alpha)`.  Since :math:`\exp()` is always positive, we
can ignore the sign and the floating point form is

.. math::

   \exp(x) &= 2^k (1 + \alpha) \\
   \Rightarrow x &= \log (2^k (1 + \alpha)) \\
      &= k \log 2 + \log(1 + \alpha)
