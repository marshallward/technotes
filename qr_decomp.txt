QR Decomposition

Basic Understanding
===================

What is QR decomposition?
-------------------------

Divide a matrix into an *orthonormal matrix* :math:`Q` and an upper triangular
matrix :math:`R`.

...and what is an orthonormal matrix?
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

:math:`Q` is *orthonormal* if it preserves dot products after vector
transformation.  That is,

.. math::

   Q \mathbf{u} \cdot Q \mathbf{v} = \mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v}

Write the dot product as a transpose, and

.. math::

   \mathbf{u}^T Q^T Q \mathbf{v} = \mathbf{u}^T \mathbf{v}

That is,

.. math::

   \mathbf{u}^T \left(Q^T Q - I\right) \mathbf{v} = 0

Or, in its more common form, :math:`Q^T Q = I`.
