How do you handle numerical stability in large-scale computations? Are there
specific tools or practices you use to detect and mitigate instability?

* In our field, stability is mostly based on timestep

  We have timestep controls and also detect instability

* We also require <10-8 precision in some operations
  
  * physical argument
  * remapping operation!

  Numerical noise must be eliminated!

  Expressions are carefully parenthesized to ensure value is preserved

* We do fixed-precision summation for global metrics, accuracy is cited as 1 in
  10^40. (Actual number depends on # of bins and bits etc)
