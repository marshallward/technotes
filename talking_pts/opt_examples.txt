ANN performance
---------------

Investigated multiple implementations of an ANN in MOM6.

Ran performance test on each version, concluded that there was no need for
custom functions and one was clearly optimal.

Cruicial observations:

* Many were doing in-place summation across the inner loop, this was
  preventing pipelined parallelization within the CPU.

  do i:
    do o:
      do s:
         y(s,o) = y(o,s) + A(i,o) x(i) 

  Parallel (in-core) sums of s were optimal

MPI
---

* Solved major scaling bottleneck in Australia MOM5 model
  Identified all-to-all as point-to-point which was bringing down the machine!

  Replaced with MPI_Alltoall intrinsic, moved problem to fabric and solved the
  scaling block: from <500 CPUs to >40k (max machine).

  * Additional speedups

    * Introduced MPI datatypes to improve data transfers (TODO?)

* Parallel netcdf implementation in GFDL FMS library, 80x speedup of
  single-file IO.

* (Minor) replaced manual bitcounts with intrinsics (popcnt)
  >10x speedup of debugging runs
